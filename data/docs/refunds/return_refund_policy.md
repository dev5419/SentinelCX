# SentinelCX return and refund policy

## Category
refunds

## Return window and eligibility
Refund and return requests must be submitted within 14 days of the confirmed delivery date. This return window applies to delivered items, including electronic goods. Damaged or defective delivered products can be submitted for a refund eligibility check under these same rules.

Only the account holder who placed the order may request a refund. The customer account must be verified and in good standing. Orders must have been successfully delivered and must have a confirmed delivery date. Cancelled orders, undelivered orders, and items that have already been refunded are not eligible. Requests outside the 14-day delivery window are not eligible.

## Automatic refunds and supervisor approval
Eligible refunds up to and including Rs 2,000 are automatically approved. Eligible refund requests exceeding Rs 2,000 require supervisor review and approval. An Order ID is needed to check the eligibility of a specific transaction. Asking about the policy does not initiate a refund.

## Refund processing timeline
Refund requests are typically processed within 5-7 business days from submission. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Weekends, holidays, request volume, and payment method can affect processing time. Contact support if there is no update after 7 business days.

## Where to find this policy
This official SentinelCX return window, eligibility, threshold, and refund processing FAQ is available through the customer support chatbot.
